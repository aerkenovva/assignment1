Discover Untold Stories & Hidden Places

Welcome to Unseen Kazakhstan a travel platform dedicated to revealing the rare, pristine, and extraordinary places of Kazakhstan. From volcanic mountains in the steppe to ancient chalk canyons left by prehistoric oceans, explore locations beyond traditional tourist itineraries.

Featured Hidden Locations

Bektau-Ata Volcanic Oasis

A volcanic granite massif situated in the middle of the Kazakh steppe near Balkhash. Famous for its smooth stone waves and deep caves containing fresh water springs.

Bozzhyra Tract

Located on the Ustyurt Plateau in Mangystau, this white chalk canyon features towering limestone castles created by the ancient Tethys Ocean millions of years ago.

Popular Regions to Uncover

Plan your journey across key natural reserves and regional territories:

  1. Mangystau Region: Martian landscapes, salt flats, and white canyons.
  2. Karaganda Region: Granite oases, volcanic formations, and steppe lakes.
  3. East Kazakhstan: Dense pine forests, alpine rivers, and Altai mountains.
  4. Turkistan Region: Ancient architecture, sacred caves, and mountain gorges.
