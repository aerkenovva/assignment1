Unexplored Locations of Kazakhstan

Discover pristine natural monuments, alien-like landscapes, and sacred places hidden across Kazakhstan's vast regions.

01. Bektau-Ata Volcanic Oasis

Region: Karaganda Region | Category: Nature & Granite Formations

A unique volcanic mountain range rising from the flat Kazakh steppe. Formed by an unexploded ancient volcano, Bektau-Ata is famous for its smooth granite waves, deep stone hollows, and fresh water caves.

Key Highlights & Features:

  1. The Sacred Cave: Deep natural cavern with an underground fresh water source.
  2. Granite Waves: Smooth rock formations carved by millions of years of wind erosion.
  3. Flora & Fauna: Rare mountain plants growing inside stone hollows.

02. Bozzhyra Tract (Ustyurt Plateau)

Region: Mangystau Region | Category: Desert & Chalk Canyons

Located on the Ustyurt Plateau, Bozzhyra features towering white chalk cliffs and limestone spires. Millions of years ago, this territory was the bottom of the prehistoric Tethys Ocean.

Key Highlights & Features:

  • The Fangs Peak: Iconic twin chalk towers overlooking the vast desert basin.
  • Ancient Fossils: Prehistoric shark teeth and shell fossils scattered along the canyon floor.
  • Stargazing Camps: Clear desert sky views with zero light pollution.

03. Akkergeshen Plateau

Region: Atyrau Region | Category: White Chalk Formations

A snow-white mountain landscape created by ancient ocean deposits. Known for its surreal, moon-like scenery and natural chalk sculptures.

Best Time to Visit:

May to September (off-road 4x4 vehicles are strongly recommended).

04. Sunken Forest of Kaindy Lake

Region: Almaty Region | Category: Alpine Lakes & Mountain Forests

Kaindy Lake is a 400-meter-long alpine lake located in the Kungey Alatau mountains. Formed after a 1911 earthquake, the lake is world-famous for the preserved trunks of Schrenk's spruce trees rising directly out of the turquoise water.

Key Highlights & Features:

  1. Underwater Forest: Perfectly preserved spruce needles beneath the icy mountain water.
  2. Turquoise Water: Vivid blue and green colors caused by lime and mineral deposits.
  3. Mountain Gorge Hike: Scenic walking route surrounded by Rocky Mountains and pine trees.
